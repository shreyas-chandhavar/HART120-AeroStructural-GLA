01 · AEROSPACE / AEROELASTICITY / LOAD ALLEVIATION

HART-120 Flexible High-Aspect-Ratio Wing

Original fictional transport-wing study built around a real aerospace systems problem: higher aspect ratio reduces induced drag but increases structural mass, flexibility and gust/maneuver loads. I coupled reduced-order aerodynamics, beam/wingbox sizing, passive aeroelastic redistribution, active load alleviation, fail-safe sizing, mission analysis, ML surrogates and Monte Carlo robustness verification.

PythonAeroelasticityGust Load AlleviationWingbox sizingMachine LearningMonte Carlo
−8.36%Induced drag
−2.01%3000-km cruise fuel
98%Joint robustness

Engineering conclusion: the final AR 15.5 concept closes the project-level structural, mission and robustness gates only after fail-safe structure, technology-system mass and uncertainty are included. ML accelerates screening; finalists are returned to the transient physics model for verification.

02 · OFFSHORE WIND / AERO-STRUCTURAL OPTIMIZATION

AeroStruct-15MW

Independent reduced-order study built around the public IEA 15-MW reference turbine. I developed and validated a BEM aerodynamic model, coupled it to rotating-beam structural analysis, generated a physics design database, trained ML surrogates, searched 100,000 candidate designs and re-verified the Pareto-selected solutions with the physics model.

PythonBEMRotating-beam FEMMachine LearningPareto optimizationIEA 15-MW
−5.65%Rated root moment
−7.23%Rated rotating tip deflection
−0.078%Aerodynamic AEP proxy

Engineering conclusion: within the bounded reduced-order design space, the optimum moved toward smooth outboard aerodynamic load redistribution rather than additional structural material. The final candidate kept the DEL10 proxy effectively unchanged while reducing selected load and deflection metrics. Results are physics-verified within the model assumptions, not IEC/OpenFAST certification claims.
